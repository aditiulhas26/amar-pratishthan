About the trust

A Ghatkopar-based trust, working since 2004

Amar Pratishthan was registered in 2004 as a public charitable trust based in Bhim Nagar, Ghatkopar (West). What began as an education and welfare initiative for underprivileged families has grown into an organisation trusted by the BMC to run sanitation programmes at the ward level.

The trust holds provisional 12A registration and 80G approval from the Income Tax Department, and works within the framework of the Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950.

Sandesh Anand Ballal President, Amar Pratishthan

Objectives recorded in the trust deed include:

  • Free and vocational education for underprivileged children
  • Balwadi, anganwadi and adult literacy classes
  • Cleanliness campaigns and public health awareness
  • Free medical, eye and blood donation camps
  • Scholarships for meritorious students from poor families
Our work with BMC

Daily sanitation work across Mumbai's wards

Under the BMC's Swachh Mumbai Prabodhan Abhiyan and Solid Waste Management department, Amar Pratishthan's field workers carry out cleanliness work on a daily basis.

Basti Swachhata Yojana

Slum-cleanliness drive covering Wards 127 & 128 — Shivraj Dayasagar, Suraksha Nagar, Daya Sagar, Kajrolkar Society and neighbouring settlements.

Public toilet cleaning

Daily cleaning and sanitisation of public toilet blocks (PSC) in N Ward under BMC's Maintenance of Toilets programme.

Toilet block reconstruction

Recognised community-based organisation (CBO) for new RCC toilet block construction under the F/N Ward Lot-12 contract.
